### Research Focus & Scientific Pipeline

My research connects endangered botanical conservation with analytical phytochemistry, bioanalytical instrumentation, and computational drug discovery. I specialize in establishing end-to-end translational pipelines from plant tissue culture and elicitation to spectroscopic characterization and in silico therapeutic screening:

```
[Plant Tissue Culture & Botanical Conservation] 
       │
       ▼
[Elicitor-Mediated Secondary Metabolite Production (JA / SA)] 
       │
       ▼
[Spectroscopic & Chromatographic Fingerprinting (GC-MS, FT-IR)] 
       │
       ▼
[In Silico Virtual Screening & Molecular Dynamics (MD) Simulation]
```

* **Endangered Flora Conservation:** Micropropagation, somatic embryogenesis, and multiple shoot induction in *Caralluma* spp. (*C. fimbriata*, *C. bhupenderiana*) and *Bacopa monnieri*.
* **Metabolomics & Phytochemistry:** High-resolution spectroscopic fingerprinting (GC-MS, FT-IR) to identify and quantify secondary metabolites under hormonal/elicitor regulation.
* **Controlled-Environment Agriculture (CEA):** Nutrient Film Technique (NFT) hydroponics parameter modeling, tracking electrical conductivity (EC), pH, and crop yield dynamics.
* **Computational Screening & Cheminformatics:** Molecular docking (AutoDock Vina) and molecular dynamics simulations to evaluate natural product binding against therapeutic protein targets.
* **Genome Editing Foundations:** Certified hands-on training in CRISPR-Cas technology via Agri Biotech Foundation and Federation of Asian Biotech Associations (FABA).
