### Research Focus & Scientific Pipeline

My research connects endangered medicinal plant conservation with analytical metabolomics and computational drug discovery. I specialize in establishing end-to-end experimental pipelines from plant cell culture and abiotic/biotic elicitation to spectroscopic characterization and in silico therapeutic evaluation:

```
[Plant Tissue Culture & Conservation] 
       │
       ▼
[Elicitor-Mediated Bioactive Production (JA / SA)] 
       │
       ▼
[Spectroscopic & Metabolomic Fingerprinting (GC-MS, FT-IR)] 
       │
       ▼
[In Silico Virtual Screening & Molecular Dynamics (MD) Simulation]
```

* **Endangered Flora Conservation:** Micropropagation, somatic embryogenesis, and multiple shoot induction in *Caralluma* spp. (*C. fimbriata*, *C. bhupenderiana*) and *Bacopa monnieri*.
* **Metabolomics & Phytochemistry:** High-resolution spectroscopic fingerprinting (GC-MS, FT-IR) to identify and quantify secondary metabolites under hormonal/elicitor regulation.
* **Controlled-Environment Agriculture (CEA):** Nutrient Film Technique (NFT) hydroponics optimization, monitoring electrical conductivity (EC), pH, and nutrient uptake dynamics.
* **Computational Screening:** Molecular docking and molecular dynamics (MD) simulations to characterize ligand-protein interactions for natural product drug discovery.
