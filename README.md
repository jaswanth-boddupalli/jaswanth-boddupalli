## 🔬 Research Focus & Scientific Pipeline

My research connects endangered botanical conservation with analytical phytochemistry, bioanalytical instrumentation, and computational drug discovery. I specialize in establishing end-to-end translational pipelines from plant tissue culture and elicitation to spectroscopic characterization and in silico therapeutic screening:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               THE BENCH-TO-COMPUTATION RESEARCH PIPELINE               │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  [1] Plant Tissue Culture & Botanical Conservation                     │
│      • Somatic embryogenesis & shoot induction (Caralluma, Bacopa)     │
│      • Overcoming explant phenolic browning & systemic contamination   │
│                                  │                                     │
│                                  ▼                                     │
│  [2] Elicitor Regulation & Bioactive Hyperaccumulation                 │
│      • Abiotic/biotic elicitation (Jasmonic acid, Salicylic acid, NaCl)│
│      • Time-series cell suspension harvest & biomass optimization      │
│                                  │                                     │
│                                  ▼                                     │
│  [3] Chromatographic & Spectroscopic Fingerprinting                    │
│      • Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS)                    │
│      • Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FT-IR)                 │
│      • Retention index calculation & NIST spectral deconvolution       │
│                                  │                                     │
│                                  ▼                                     │
│  [4] In Silico Virtual Screening & Molecular Dynamics (MD)             │
│      • Cheminformatics (RDKit) & Lipinski Rule-of-5 drug-likeness      │
│      • AutoDock Vina molecular docking & free energy estimation (ΔG)   │
│      • Macromolecular target binding & PyMOL 3D contact visualization  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
